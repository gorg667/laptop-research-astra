THE SHORT VERSION

Compatibility first.
Enough memory. A screen you like.
A laptop you want to carry.

For most students and developers, start with 16–32 GB RAM, a 512 GB–1 TB SSD, and a good 13–14-inch display. Buy a GPU for a specific workload—not for the idea of one.
